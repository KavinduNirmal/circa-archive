# The Unwalled Door

**CIRCA-S-03007** · Filed by **Daydream Inc.** (`Qterw-7-3`) · Status: **OPEN**

---

## Brief

A free-standing door in a pasture in Gangwon Province. No walls. No frame beyond the door itself. It has been there for at least sixty years, possibly longer. It does not move. It does not open on its own. It is, by every available measure, a door.

Since 1996, records concerning individuals who have passed through the door have been found to contain inconsistencies. Not errors. **Inconsistencies.** Two birth dates. Two service records. Two entries for the same event, both correct, both on file.

Daydream Inc. first became aware of the door in 2004, following a routine audit of regional registry data. The audit flagged eleven individuals whose official records disagreed with themselves.

| Subject | Age | Discrepancy | Verified? |
|---------|-----|-------------|-----------|
| S-01 | 34 | Two birth dates (1987-04-12 / 1987-04-19) | Both |
| S-02 | 41 | Two service records (Unit 3 / Unit 7) | Both |

| S-06–S-11 | — | *Records sealed by request* | — |

All eleven grew up within twenty kilometers of the door. All eleven opened it.

## Field Account

*Filed by H. Kim, Field Officer, 2004-09-08*

I arrived in the village on a Tuesday. The door is a twenty-minute walk from the main road, past a rice paddy and a stand of pines. You can see it from a distance. It stands upright in the grass, slightly leaning, the way a door leans when its frame has settled.

![right|Figure 1. The door, photographed from the south approach, 2004. The pines are visible at the treeline.](./evidence/door-south-2004.jpg)

There is no wall. There is no frame. There is a door, and there is grass, and there is the sky above it. The hinges are rusted but functional. The handle is brass, worn smooth. The wood is dark and swollen with rain.

I asked a farmer who owns the adjacent field when the door was installed. He said it was there when his father was a boy. He said his father asked the same question. He said the answer was the same.

I opened the door.

I want to be clear about what happened next, because I have read the accounts of the eleven individuals and none of them agree. One says they saw a room. One says they saw a field identical to the one they were standing in. One says they saw nothing, only a brightness. One says they saw the inside of their own childhood home. One says they saw their own back, walking away.

I saw a corridor. It was not a long corridor. It was the kind of corridor you find in an office building — fluorescent lighting, gray carpet, a door at the far end with a small window. There was no one in it. I stepped through.

![full|Figure 2. Interior view. Photograph taken from within the doorway, facing outward. The field is visible through the door. No corridor is visible.](./evidence/door-interior-2004.jpg)

I was on the other side. The other side was the same field. The same grass, the same pines, the same sky. The door was behind me. I turned around and opened it again and stepped through and I was back where I started. Nothing had changed.

I filed my report. I went home. I did not think about it again for six weeks.

Six weeks later, I received a notice from the regional registry. My birth record had been amended. My date of birth was listed as `1987-04-12`. It had always been `1987-04-12`. But the record also listed a second date of birth, `1987-04-19`. Both were marked as verified. Both were marked as original. The registry could not explain the discrepancy. It could not explain why the discrepancy had appeared only after my visit.

I have not been back to the door. I do not intend to.

> **NOTICE TO CORRESPONDENTS**
> This record describes an observed phenomenon. It does not confirm the phenomenon's nature, origin, or intent. Correspondents are advised that the door in question is ||not to be approached|| and that any personal records found to contain inconsistencies should be reported to the registry immediately.
>

## Cross-Reference

**Filed by:** Supernatural Disaster Management Bureau (`1793P-SYA.2004.파09`)

> The Bureau has reviewed the Daydream Inc. report and finds its conclusions premature. The door is not an *incident*. It is a ||disaster||. The distinction is not semantic.
>
> The Bureau's own investigation, conducted in 2001, identified fourteen individuals with inconsistent records. The Bureau's report was filed under a separate code and was not shared with Daydream Inc. The Bureau does not consider the door to be a Daydream matter.
>
> The Bureau's position is that the door is a ||containment breach|| and should be ||sealed||. The Bureau has filed a request with the Commission for Incident Review and Corrective Action to reclassify the incident from `CIRCA-S` to `CIRCA-A`. The request is pending.

## Addendum

*Added 2004-09-14, following review*

The eleven individuals identified in the original audit have since been contacted. All eleven report the same experience: **they have no memory of the door.** They do not remember opening it. They do not remember the field, the pines, the grass. When shown photographs of the door, they do not recognize it. When told they grew up near it, they do not believe it.

Their records remain inconsistent. The registry has not resolved the discrepancies. The Commission has taken no action.

The door is still there. It is still a door. It is still in the field.

If you go through it, please file a report.
