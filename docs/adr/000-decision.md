# NNN: Short title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Maintainers
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

What is true now that forces a decision. Name the constraints, the forces in tension, and what is already built. Link the code, documents, or external sources that carry the evidence. A reader who was not in the discussion should be able to tell why the decision was needed at all.

## Decision

State the decision in the present tense — "We do X" — and keep it to one paragraph. Then list the rules that make it checkable, if the decision has parts. A reader should be able to say whether a proposed change complies.

## Consequences

**Positive.** What this buys, concretely.

**Negative.** What it costs, what it forecloses, and what becomes harder. An ADR with no negative consequences has not been thought through.

**Follow-ups.** What must become true later, and what would trigger a revisit. Include the condition that would justify a superseding ADR.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative.** What it would have looked like, and why it was not chosen.
- **Do nothing.** Why the status quo was not viable.

## References

- Repository code and documents that carry the evidence.
- External specifications or vendor documentation, with links.
