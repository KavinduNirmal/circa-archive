# Contribution

The archive grows through writing, code, corrections, and careful review. Start with the guide that matches your change.

## Write within the setting

Read the [Author guide](../../AUTHOR_GUIDE.md) for authorship, permissions, evidence, and continuity decisions. Consult the [Lore overview](../../lore/README.md) for the public setting. These documents describe policy and fiction; they do not imply that publication and moderation workflows are implemented.

## Prepare a record

[Writing a record](records.md) covers Markdown, frontmatter, classifications, redactions, and figures. The archive currently presents illustrative fixtures.

## Change the project

[Contributing](contributing.md) explains focused changes, repository conventions, verification, and pull requests. For local setup and the implementation, continue to [Building](building.md).
