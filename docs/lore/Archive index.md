# The archive index

The archive's Incident index begins at **INC-07539**.

That is the beginning available to the archive. It is not the beginning of Ghost Stories.

## The missing beginning

Incidents have occurred throughout history. Their origin and the cause of their existence remain unknown. The archive does not predate them.

CIRCA understands that earlier records must have existed. Those records are not filed here. Their contents cannot be supplied from the gap in the index.

An absent record does not establish an absent occurrence.

## References and chronology

An archive reference identifies what is being cited. Its number alone should not be used to establish the age of a phenomenon or the order in which every surviving reference came into use.

The index begins at INC-07539. Later references include [The Recollection Projector](Recollection%20Projector.md), designated INC-E-10743.

The starting reference does not supply the contents of missing earlier records or establish a founding history.
