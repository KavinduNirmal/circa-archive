# Narrative flow

Every [Incident](Anatomy.md) has a beginning and a defined conclusion. Its completion conditions determine how the story can end.

A conclusion may require an action, an outcome, or the passage of time. A story that concludes only after a hundred years still has a completion condition. Difficulty reaching the end does not establish that no end exists.

## Reading the ending

The themes of an [anchor](Anchors.md), the behavior of the nature, and the objectives of the actors can provide clues to the conclusion.

A story may begin with a girl visiting her grandmother and move toward the wolf eating her. An explorer may find a permitted loophole that produces another ending, perhaps by outsmarting the wolf. A different ending still concludes the story; it is not a withdrawal from participation.

A permitted alternative conclusion exploits the operative rules. It does not authorize an actor to rewrite the underlying ending because the actor dislikes it. [Participation and completion requirements](Rules.md#intelligence-and-its-limits) can be mutable while the story and actor limitations remain constrained.

Record the conditions supporting an alternative conclusion. A familiar tale may suggest a question without establishing every rule of the Incident encountered.

## Competing actors

An Incident may have several primary actors with contradictory objectives. The victorious actor concludes the story according to its ending.

An actor may also be unaware that it belongs to an Incident. Some natures faithfully imitate ordinary reality, without the appearance by which an explorer expects to recognize a Ghost Story.

Destroying a primary actor does not always end the nature. If that actor is not pivotal to completion, the system can remain active. Some natures replace a destroyed actor.

## Clearance and recurrence

**Clearance** is the end of the current story. The stage resets after the conclusion, allowing another run when the participants have left and the entry conditions are met again.

Some Incidents have a cooldown or delay before reappearance. The next run begins at the beginning, with participants again approaching a conclusion. [Adaptive or intelligent phenomena](Phenomena.md) may change what is encountered on later runs, within the system’s [limits of alteration](Rules.md#intelligence-and-its-limits). A later surprise does not establish unrestricted rewriting of the story.

A cleared Incident is therefore not necessarily destroyed or permanently inaccessible. Record the ending reached, the reset, and any delay separately.

## Commitment

Once admitted, a [participant](Participation.md) cannot refuse the story. The way forward is through its conclusion. Outsmarting an actor can change the ending when the system permits it; simply deciding to stop does not end participation.
