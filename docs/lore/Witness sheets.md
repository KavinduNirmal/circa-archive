# Witness sheets

An explorer need not survive for their recorded experience to reach the archive.

CIRCA uses three class E Incidents together to preserve and review that experience: [The Witness Pen](Witness%20Pen.md), [The Lasting Bamboo](Lasting%20Bamboo.md), and [The Recollection Projector](Recollection%20Projector.md).

## Preparation and recording

The Witness Pen, INC-E-08932, is used to draw an eye and accompanying marks on a four-inch-square sheet of bamboo paper cut from INC-E-10534.

The prepared sheet records the vision of the person carrying it. This marked paper is referred to here as a **witness sheet**.

## After death

The paper cannot be destroyed and survives the Ghost Stories in which it is carried. If its carrier dies, their recorded experience is still recovered.

This preserves an account from an exploration that left no surviving narrator. It does not require the dead explorer to compose a report afterward.

## Playback

The sheet is put through INC-E-10743, the Recollection Projector. The recorded vision is projected with sound and can be reviewed for the archive.

A written [record](Records.md) can cite that projection as its source. Keep the carrier's experience distinct from the analyst's later interpretation.

Playback ordinarily remains outside the active Incident. Rare [awareness-triggered encounters](Participation.md#exceptional-participation) require separate consideration. Recovery of a recording is not a live indication of the carrier's condition, particularly where [time differs](Time.md) inside and outside.

## Limits of the account

Playback faithfully preserves perception, including hallucinations, manipulated memories, and sensory distortions. It supplies no unobserved perspective. A recording that survives intact can therefore remain difficult to interpret.

This arrangement preserves what was recorded. It does not establish that the explorer understood the event, that every part of the Incident was observed, or that the expedition achieved its objective.

The recovery of a recording is also distinct from the recovery of a body or a living person.

CIRCA's [warning about identification](Anonymity.md) still applies. Recording an encounter does not make the carrier unobservable to the Ghost Story.
