# The Lasting Bamboo

**Archive designation:** INC-E-10534

**Classification:** [E](Classifications.md#class-e)

**Form:** Bamboo source for recording paper

Bamboo paper cut from INC-E-10534 cannot be destroyed. It survives the Ghost Stories into which it is carried.

The CIRCA archive uses four-inch-square sheets in its [recording arrangement](Witness%20sheets.md). An eye and accompanying marks drawn with [The Witness Pen](Witness%20Pen.md) allow a sheet to record its carrier's vision.

## What survives

The paper's endurance preserves the recording when the explorer carrying it dies. The explorer's recorded experience is recovered even when the explorer cannot return to give an account.

The survival of the sheet is not the survival of its carrier. A recovered recording and a rescued person must be accounted for separately.
