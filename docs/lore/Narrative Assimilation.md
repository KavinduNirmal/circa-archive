# Narrative Assimilation

**Medical and investigative reference**

Narrative Assimilation is an illustrative progression used by CIRCA medical and investigative personnel to describe changes associated with [corruption](Corruption.md).

Other organizations may name those changes differently. The progression is not a universally reliable scale.

## Reference stages

| Stage   | Name              | Reference value | Described signs                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------- | ----------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **0**   | **Baseline**      | **5%**          | No established signs of supernatural contamination.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **I**   | **Residual**      | **22%**         | Recurring dreams, anomalous familiarity, or sensitivity to supernatural anchors.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **II**  | **Displacement**  | **43%**         | Inconsistent memories, altered personal records, or unexplained knowledge.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **III** | **Assimilation**  | **63%**         | The explorer begins exhibiting characteristics of previously encountered stories.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **IV**  | **Incorporation** | **82%**         | Ordinary reality begins treating the explorer as an entity or as part of an existing story.                                                                                                                                         |
| **V**   | **Manifestation** | **100%**        | The person behaves as an actor of the Incident that corrupted them and may remain in ordinary reality as an autonomous entity. Establishing an independent Incident additionally requires a new anchor and complete narrative flow. |

The percentages are reference values within the illustration. They are not measurements of a person's condition, probabilities of transformation, or thresholds by which an outcome can be predicted.

Baseline's reference value does not establish contamination. The sequence does not establish that every case passes through every stage in the same order.

## Early response notice

If the described signs are noticed, immediately cease the current activity and suspend further exploration or use of anomalous recoveries. Isolate the recoveries from continued use. Take leave for mental recovery and consult the counsellor provided by your organization.

Preserve an account of recent Incidents, the duration of exposure, and the recoveries used. Include when the changes were first noticed and who observed them.

During an active encounter, ceasing optional activity does not cancel participation or create an exit. The story must still reach its conclusion.

This notice calls for assessment. A suspected sign does not establish a stage, and leave or counselling does not, by itself, establish that corruption has been reversed.

## Continued life and employment

A corrupted person may remain in ordinary reality, behaving as an actor of the source story. Seeking return to that Incident is a severe presentation, not the normal consequence of Stage V.

Organizations may continue employing corrupted personnel under their own policies. CIRCA records those policies without endorsing them. Remaining employed does not establish reversal of corruption or absence of risk.

## Severe cases

Containment and intensive rehabilitation are recommended in severe cases, including active, compulsive return-seeking. Stage V status alone is not the severe-return presentation. Where rehabilitation is not feasible, neutralization is recommended.

The recommendation concerns the individual's assessed condition. A reference percentage or an isolated symptom is not sufficient to establish that condition or the feasibility of rehabilitation.

Record the observations, the assessment, and the response as separate parts of the account. Practical measures remain the responsibility of the responding organization.
