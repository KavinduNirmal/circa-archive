# Entry

The method of entering a [Ghost Story](Anomalies.md) is bound to its [anchor](Anchors.md). How a person enters matters to what can happen to them and what they can bring back.

## Physical and nonphysical entry

Physical entry into a separate space is common. Some Incidents are entered spiritually or during sleep instead.

These encounters are not without consequence because the body remains elsewhere. Death inside such a Ghost Story also results in the death of the physical body.

A sleeping body is not evidence of an unexposed person. An account should distinguish the condition of the body from what the explorer experienced within the Incident.

## Awareness as entry

Rare [participation conditions](Participation.md#exceptional-participation) make observing a Ghost Story or becoming aware of it sufficient to enter the encounter. Physical distance from its original space is not a dependable boundary in those cases.

## Binding participation

Once the system admits a person, they cannot withdraw from participation. Admission may be unrecognized or accidental; the story must still reach a [conclusion](Narrative%20flow.md). A declaration of intent is required only where the particular conditions demand it.

A participant cannot be in several Incidents simultaneously except through [nested entry](Nested%20incidents.md) via an anchor inside the first story.

## Carried possessions

In ordinary entry, items, wearables, and other possessions carried by the explorer enter with them.

Record what was carried and what returned. Possession before entry does not establish that the same item remained available throughout the encounter.

## Role-based entry

Rare Incidents require the explorer to perform as the story's central protagonist or actor. The explorer may be transformed to fit the nature of that story.

This required role is distinct from the [primary actor](Phenomena.md#nature-and-primary-actor) identified in an account of the phenomenon. The person entering may be made to occupy a role they did not previously possess.

Ordinary carried items may not survive that change. In such cases, possessions may need to be carried within special [recoveries](Recoveries.md) to survive entry.

The conditions of any such recovery require their own identification. Do not assume an ordinary container provides the same protection.

The [witness sheet's](Witness%20sheets.md) established endurance remains distinct from the survival of ordinary possessions.

## Death and claimed protection

Death within an Incident kills the explorer. Spiritual or sleep entry does not provide an exception.

Rare recoveries are said to save their users from an otherwise fatal experience. Such a claim requires careful identification of the item and its usage conditions before it is relied upon.

A claim of protection does not establish that every death can be reversed, or that the conditions of one recovery apply to another.

[Survival, escape, and clearance](Outcomes.md) must still be recorded separately.
