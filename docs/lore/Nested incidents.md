# Nested Incidents

The spaces of Ghost Stories belong to a connected universe. An explorer may find the anchor of one Incident within another, or rarely encounter actors originating from a different story.

Connected spaces do not make every Incident the same system. Identify each [anchor](Anchors.md), its entry conditions, and the story to which it belongs.

## Participation within participation

A person ordinarily cannot participate in more than one Incident at the same time.

Nested entry is the exception: a participant in Incident A encounters an anchor for Incident B within it and enters the second system. Record both connections rather than describing an ordinary second admission from outside.

Finding an anchor is not, by itself, proof that its entry conditions have been met. Nor does entry into the nested story establish clearance of the surrounding one.

## Actors and absorption

An actor from another story may appear within the nature being explored. Its presence does not automatically make that actor the host Incident's primary actor or establish that the two systems have merged.

In extremely rare cases, one Incident absorbs another. Such an account must distinguish the systems involved from their actors and preserve what supports the claimed absorption.

An apparent connection is a reason to investigate the relationship. It does not supply the relationship's entire history.
