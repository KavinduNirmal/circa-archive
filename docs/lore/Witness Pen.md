# The Witness Pen

**Archive designation:** INC-E-08932

**Classification:** [E](Classifications.md#class-e)

**Form:** Fountain pen

The Witness Pen is used by the CIRCA archive to prepare [witness sheets](Witness%20sheets.md).

An eye and a set of accompanying marks are drawn with the pen on a four-inch-square sheet of bamboo paper cut from [INC-E-10534](Lasting%20Bamboo.md). The marked sheet records the vision of the person carrying it.

The eye is part of the recording arrangement. It should not be taken as an indication that the sheet sees independently of its carrier.

## The recorded account

The arrangement preserves experience that an explorer may not survive to describe. The recording can be reviewed through [INC-E-10743](Recollection%20Projector.md), which projects the recorded vision with sound.

The pen supplies a means of recording. It does not supply an interpretation of what has been recorded.
