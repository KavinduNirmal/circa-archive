# Phenomena

A Ghost Story may contain a presence that acts with intention. It may also proceed without any apparent awareness of the people encountering it.

Neither a familiar face nor an answering voice is sufficient to settle the distinction.

## Nature and primary actor

The **nature** of a Ghost Story is the setting and surrounding behavior that fit its narrative. In an abandoned grocery store, the premises may provide that setting while a ghost acts within it.

A **primary actor** is a principal acting presence in the encounter. It may be that ghost or the store itself. Several primary actors may pursue contradictory objectives; the victorious actor concludes the story according to its ending.

The distinction is descriptive. A location-based Incident need not have a hidden individual directing it, and an actor need not possess a consciousness merely because its behavior resembles a role.

In rare [role-based Incidents](Entry.md#role-based-entry), the explorer must perform as a protagonist or actor and may be transformed to fit. Identify that imposed role separately from the acting presence described in earlier accounts.

Some [autonomous actors](Independent%20entities.md) can act outside their source stories without ending those systems. An emancipated actor may persist after its source becomes inaccessible. Neither is automatically an independent Incident; that development requires a new anchor and a complete narrative flow.

An actor may be unaware of its role, especially where the nature convincingly imitates ordinary reality. A nature can persist after a non-pivotal actor is destroyed, and some natures replace destroyed primary actors. Review the [narrative flow](Narrative%20flow.md) before treating an actor's death as clearance.

Actors from other stories may rarely appear within a [connected or nested Incident](Nested%20incidents.md).

## Observed behavior

| Description       | Observations                                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reactive**      | Responds to conditions without apparent awareness.       |
| **Adaptive**      | Changes behavior in response to repeated investigations. |
| **Intelligent**   | Demonstrates memory, intention, and strategic behavior.  |
| **Indeterminate** | Shows inconsistent signs of awareness.                   |

Describe the evidence for the term used. These descriptions do not form a ladder that every Incident must climb. An adaptive response does not, on its own, establish an intelligent actor.

## Repeated encounters

A known route or reset procedure may cease to produce the earlier result. Some phenomena adapt after repeated resets without deaths, or when the required reset conditions have not been met.

An earlier account remains evidence of that encounter. It is not a promise about the next one.

Record what was attempted, whether the required conditions were met, and what changed. Do not treat every failed repetition as proof that the phenomenon has learned; an earlier understanding of its conditions may also have been incomplete.

## Limits of adaptation

Changes remain governed by [rules](Rules.md). Intelligent phenomena may alter participation rules or completion requirements; they cannot ordinarily rewrite their underlying story, ending, or the behavior and limitations of an actor.

Higher-classification exceptions require specific evidence. Recognizing a repeated strategy does not itself authorize an actor to act outside its limits.

## Assessment

An intelligent phenomenon can be difficult to clear despite a low recorded death toll. Its ability to remember explorers or change its behavior matters alongside the casualties already on file.

An [Incident classification](Classifications.md) may therefore lag behind the phenomenon it describes. Familiarity with the file should not be mistaken for familiarity with everything that can now occur.

CIRCA's [warning against identification](Anonymity.md) remains in effect. Not knowing whether something is aware does not establish that it cannot recognize an explorer.
