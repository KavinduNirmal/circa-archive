# Lore documentation

## Responsibility

CIRCA's original fictional setting and shared vocabulary. The reference notes use the Commission's restrained archival voice: an account can be preserved without its conclusions being accepted.

## Structure

- [Anatomy](Anatomy.md): the Incident system, its actors, nature, anchors, and conditions; includes the supplied reference diagram.
- [Rules](Rules.md): operative rules, unreliable statements, semantics, applicability, and the limits of intelligent alteration.
- [Narrative flow](Narrative%20flow.md): beginnings, endings, competing actors, clearance, and recurrence.
- [Nested Incidents](Nested%20incidents.md): connected spaces, cross-story actors and anchors, and overlapping participation.
- [C.I.R.C.A.](C.I.R.C.A.md): the Commission, its uncertain origin, and the limits of its work.
- [On the Nature of Ghost Stories](Anomalies.md): Incidents, observed rules, separate spaces, and the limits of explanation.
- [Phenomena](Phenomena.md): nature, primary actors, awareness, and adaptation between encounters.
- [Independent entities](Independent%20entities.md): actors in ordinary reality, extracted entities, and institutional service.
- [Incident classifications](Classifications.md): harmlessness, casualties, resolution difficulty, and exploration guidance.
- [Anchors](Anchors.md): connections to ordinary reality, entrances, and manifestation surfaces.
- [Entry](Entry.md): physical, spiritual, sleep, and role-based entry; possessions and fatal consequences.
- [Time](Time.md): internal duration, external absence, and the limits of rescue assumptions.
- [Participation](Participation.md): anchor-bound reach, outside review, and exceptional entry through observation or awareness.
- [Outcomes](Outcomes.md): survival, escape, clearance, reset, suppression, resolution, and evidence-based assessment.
- [Archive index](Archive%20index.md): INC-07539, missing earlier records, and the limits of numbering as history.
- [Explorations](Explorations.md): field observations and the distinction between survival, understanding, and completion.
- [Civilians](Civilians.md): witness knowledge, consultation, secrecy, and rescue.
- [Anonymity](Anonymity.md): reciprocal observation and CIRCA’s warning against identification.
- [Recoveries](Recoveries.md): residual, active, and suspected anchor recoveries.
- [Corruption](Corruption.md): cumulative exposure and changes that may persist after departure.

- [The Lasting Bamboo](Lasting%20Bamboo.md): INC-E-10534, the source of indestructible recording paper.

- [Continuities](Continuities.md): branches of recorded history and institutional persistence.

Read the Commission and Ghost Stories notes first. Follow the noun links for narrower distinctions. Illustrations within these notes are not individually established case files.

## Author guidance

For real-world contribution permissions and continuity decisions, read the [Author guide](../AUTHOR_GUIDE.md). It describes platform policy rather than laws of a Ghost Story. Publication, evidentiary support, and adoption are separate decisions; the platform workflows remain unimplemented.

## Boundaries

Keep fictional knowledge separate from author explanations, API contracts, and contribution policy. These are prose references, not implemented archive records or evidence of backend behavior. Use the [project documentation](../README.md) and [draft architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) for repository and technical context.

Preserve the distinction between a phenomenon and accounts of it. Organizations may disagree; filing is not proof, recognition is not endorsement, and a primary claim is not ownership. These notes provide shared vocabulary without declaring one authoritative account of every Incident.

Use original or appropriately licensed material. Do not copy organizations, incidents, or prose from source inspirations. Link related terms and provide a README for each new authored folder.

## Usage and verification

Edit the Markdown directly. Check relative links and heading references, read related notes for consistency, and run `bun run check` from the repository root for folder README coverage and tracked whitespace checks. Lore-only edits do not require application tests.
